About Mmatle Holdings

100% Black Female & Youth-Owned Independent Environmental Excellence

Mmatle Holdings (Pty) Ltd, trading as Mmatle Environmentals, is a Level 1 B-BBEE, 100% Black female and youth-owned independent environmental and water consultancy. We possess in-depth theoretical and practical knowledge in environmental and water management.

We are committed to improving environmental sustainability, water quality, and wastewater management through practical, science-based solutions. We integrate scientific rigor with expertise in sustainability to improve public health and operational performance for municipalities, government departments, and the private sector across South Africa.

Comprehensive Service Offerings

Environmental & Wastewater Management Solutions

Science-based solutions tailored for municipalities, government departments, water boards, and private sector clients.

Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA)

Comprehensive scoping, rigorous environmental impact evaluations, and regulatory applications for infrastructure and private developments.

ECO & EO Compliance Monitoring

Professional Environmental Control Officer (ECO) and Environmental Officer (EO) monitoring to ensure strict site adherence to conditions of approval.

WWRAP Development & Review

Specialized Wastewater Risk Abatement Plan (WWRAP) development and reviews for municipal wastewater treatment works and districts.

Green Drop Readiness Assessments

Evaluating wastewater system performance against Department of Water and Sanitation (DWS) Green Drop certification criteria.

Water Quality Audits & Lab Support

Drinking and borehole water audits, laboratory data interpretation, quality systems development, WSP implementation, and sampling.

GIS Mapping & Water Use Licensing

Advanced spatial GIS mapping, Water Use License Applications (WULA), borehole assessments, and mining rights applications.
